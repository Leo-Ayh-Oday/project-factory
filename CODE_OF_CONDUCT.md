# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

**Positive behavior:** empathy, respect, constructive feedback, accountability, community-first thinking.

**Unacceptable behavior:** sexualized language/imagery, trolling, harassment, publishing private info, unprofessional conduct.

## Enforcement

Report to [2115464137@qq.com](mailto:2115464137@qq.com). All complaints reviewed promptly and fairly.
